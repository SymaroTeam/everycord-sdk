# Changelog

All notable changes to the EveryCord SDKs and the Partner API. The full notes are in
[docs/changelog.md](docs/changelog.md) and at [everycord.net/developers/changelog](https://everycord.net/developers/changelog).

## Unreleased: early access

- Documentation for the iOS and Android SDKs, the Partner API, webhooks, tiers and branding, earnings and the partner
  dashboard.
- `Package.swift` and `EveryCordSDK.podspec` for the iOS release, and the Android install notes.
- The first release, 1.0.0, attaches `EveryCordSDK.xcframework.zip` (iOS, Swift Package Manager),
  `EveryCordSDK-CocoaPods.zip` (iOS, CocoaPods: the same framework with LiveKit's `RustLiveKitUniFFI.xcframework`) and
  the AAR with its POM (Android).
